Click for Live Video!

Live video of MyEncinitasTV is available 24/7. Meetings will be shown during their scheduled times. If the Live Feed window is opened before the scheduled meeting time the video feed will not display automatically when the meeting begins. Please refresh your browser at the scheduled meeting time ("F5" for Windows, "Command+R" for Mac). Alternatively, you can wait until the scheduled meeting time to open the "Click for Live Video!" link above.
